Hello! I'm Minxu Liu, a Master's student in Electronic Information (Computer Technology) at NUAA, and incoming PhD student at the Zhejiang University School of Artificial Intelligence (Fall 2026). My research sits at the intersection of Deep Learning, Multimodal Learning, Neural Visual Decoding, and Affective Computing.

I focus on decoding the human visual cortex from EEG signals — recovering what people see from brain activity — and on robust multi-modal emotion recognition across individuals. My work has been accepted at top venues including ICML 2026 (CCF-A) and published in IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems (JCR Q1).

ICML 2026 Accepted · CCF-A #01
ViEEG: Hierarchical Visual Neural Representation for EEG Brain Decoding
Liu Minxu et al.
International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026 — Dec 2024 → Apr 2025
Semantically segments visual images into a contour → foreground → full-image hierarchy to restore the human brain's layered visual understanding. Reconstructs hierarchical visual information via diffusion models. Achieves a landmark improvement on the large-scale public neural signal decoding dataset (zero-shot image retrieval: 26% → 40%).

👁
TSMCA — JCR Q1 Under Review · IF 8.7 #02
EEG Spatial-Temporal Topology Network for Brain Visual Decoding
Liu Minxu et al.
IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems — Jul 2024 → Dec 2024
Introduces topological connectivity to explicitly model brain topology strength when receiving visual stimuli. Proposes a feature-dimensionality-reduction-based soft alignment to narrow the heterogeneity between EEG and vision-language large model representations. Designs a task-oriented representation disentanglement module for simultaneous coarse-grained object recognition and fine-grained image reconstruction.
⚡
KDD 2026 Under Review · CCF-A #03
VISTA: Dynamic Spatial-Temporal Asynchrony for EEG Visual Decoding
Liu Minxu et al.
SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (KDD) 2026 — Apr 2025 → Aug 2025
Disentangles visual-stimulus-evoked EEG fluctuations into fine-grained visual understanding and coarse-grained semantic understanding. Models the spatiotemporal dynamic asynchrony of visual and semantic modal information simultaneously in temporal and spatial dimensions, providing interpretability for EEG-based brain visual decoding.

IEEE TCDS — JCR Q1 Published · IF 5.0 #04
Multi-Modal Discriminative Network for Emotion Recognition across Individuals
Liu Minxu et al.
IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems 2025 — Oct 2023 → Apr 2024
Leverages attention and contrastive learning to mine heterogeneous and complementary representations in multimodal signals, fusing different representations for robust multimodal emotion recognition. Designs an embedded self-representation learning network using constrained block-diagonal matrices to facilitate invariant emotion representation mining across subjects.

ICME 2026 Accepted · CCF-B #05
Non-Depersonalized Disentanglement for Cross-Subject Emotion Recognition
Liu Minxu et al.
IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME) 2026 — Jun 2024 → Nov 2024
Addresses the problem that marginal probability alignment in cross-subject tasks eliminates personalized emotional features. Designs an intra-domain and cross-domain invariant representation disentanglement module with separation constraints; optimizes spectral-spatial attention networks via topological connectivity.
